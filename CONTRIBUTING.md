# Feedback & Community Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in **KIM Android Apps**! All applications in this ecosystem are distributed as **free-to-use proprietary freeware**.

---

## 💡 How to Contribute

While the underlying application source code is maintained privately by the author to prevent unauthorized clones, community feedback, bug reports, and ideas are warmly welcomed!

### 1. 🐛 Reporting Bugs
If you encounter a bug, crash, or unexpected behavior in any of our 28+ applications:
- Open a [Bug Report](https://github.com/eliakimrosil/kim-android-apps/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- Specify the application name, version tag, your Android device model, and steps to reproduce.

### 2. ✨ Feature Requests
Have an idea to make an existing offline utility even better?
- Open a [Feature Request](https://github.com/eliakimrosil/kim-android-apps/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
- Detail how your suggestion adheres to our 100% offline-first, zero-data privacy philosophy.

### 3. 🚀 New App Proposals
Have an idea for a brand new offline utility or domain sentinel?
- Submit a [New App Proposal](https://github.com/eliakimrosil/kim-android-apps/issues/new?template=new_app_proposal.yml).

### 4. 🔒 Security Vulnerabilities
If you discover a security flaw or unexpected permission leak, please disclose it privately per our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) via email to **eliakimrosil@gmail.com**.

---

## 🏛️ Guiding Standards for All Applications
- **Pure Native**: Built with pure Android Framework components (Java + XML).
- **100% Offline**: Zero analytics, zero ad networks, zero cloud lock-in.
- **Bespoke Domain Craftsmanship**: Every app is customized for its domain with tailored palettes, tactile controls, and adaptive vector icons.
