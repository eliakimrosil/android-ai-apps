ClipScrub is a lightweight, on-device utility designed to sanitize shared links and remove sensitive metadata before you send content to others. Every day, web links carry invasive tracking parameters (such as UTM tags, click identifiers, and referral tokens), while camera photos contain embedded EXIF metadata, including exact GPS coordinates, device models, and timestamps. ClipScrub integrates directly into your Android share sheet to sanitize your content instantly, keeping your sharing habits private. Key Features: • Universal Link Cleaning: Strips tracking tokens, campaign parameters, and analytics tags from URLs while preserving the core destination link. • EXIF and Location Stripping: Cleans embedded GPS coordinates, camera specs, and capture timestamps from photos before they are transmitted. • Native Share Target: Works effortlessly with your existing workflow. Tap Share in any app, select ClipScrub, and forward sanitized content to your intended recipient. • Zero Network Permissions: Operates completely offline on your device. ClipScrub contains no trackers, no third-party analytics, and no remote servers. • Fast and Frictionless: Clean links and media with minimal taps, avoiding disruptive redirections and multi-step dialogs.
