FastTrack is an offline intermittent fasting tracker designed for privacy, speed, and simplicity. Monitor your fasting intervals without mandatory accounts, recurring subscription paywalls, or third-party data tracking. Key Features: • Offline-First Architecture: All fasting sessions, schedules, and history stay stored securely on your device. • No Account Required: Open the app and begin tracking immediately without email registration or social logins. • Quick Settings Tile: Toggle fasting states directly from your Android notification shade without opening the application. • Real-Time Chronometer: Track elapsed and remaining fasting time with a lightweight, battery-efficient status bar notification. • Standard Fasting Protocols: Support for popular schedules including 16:8, 14:10, 18:6, 20:4, and custom duration timers. • Clean History and Statistics: Review past fasts, completion rates, and streak consistency with straightforward local charts. • Minimal Battery Footprint: Operates with zero background network polling and zero analytics SDKs. Take control of your daily routine with a fast, private, and distraction-free fasting timer built specifically for Android.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
