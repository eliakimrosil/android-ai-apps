ClampGuard provides reliable, offline-first clamp countdown timers and epoxy cure calculations engineered specifically for woodworking shops and makers. Premature clamp removal ruins glue joints, while guessing cure schedules risks structural failure. ClampGuard eliminates guesswork by calculating precise open times and clamp pressure duration based on ambient workshop temperatures, adhesive chemistry, and joint requirements. Built for messy hands and workshop environments, ClampGuard requires no account creation, no internet connection, and zero intrusive distractions while you work. Key Features: • Temperature-Adjusted Cure Times: Compensates for workshop temperature fluctuations using accurate cure curve modeling for standard PVA, polyurethane, and epoxy resins. • One-Touch Quick Settings Tile: Trigger preset clamp timers directly from Android Quick Settings without navigating through menus with glue on your gloves. • Lock Screen Countdown: Track remaining clamp hold time and full cure progress via persistent lock screen chronometer notifications. • Offline-First Reliability: Operates completely offline with zero data collection, no mandatory cloud sync, and no login requirements. • Multiple Adhesive Presets: Pre-configured profiles for yellow wood glue, liquid hide glue, fast-set cyanoacrylate, structural polyurethane, and casting epoxies. • Multi-Clamp Project Tracking: Monitor multiple glue-ups, laminations, and resin pours concurrently with custom project tags. • Configurable Safety Margins: Add buffer times for stressed joints, high-humidity environments, or bent laminations. Work with precision and protect your woodworking projects from joint failure.
