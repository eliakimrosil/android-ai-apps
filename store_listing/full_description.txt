AuraFocus is a minimalist, offline-first productivity timer designed for deep work, study sessions, and uninterrupted flow. Eliminate digital fatigue with a streamlined workflow that respects your attention and device resources. Key Features: • Flexible Interval Timing: Customize work intervals, short breaks, and long breaks based on the proven Pomodoro technique to maintain consistent productivity. • Curated Ambient Audio: Blend calming, high-fidelity soundscapes including rain, forest, white noise, and gentle streams directly on your device. • Offline and Private: All countdowns, session logs, and audio playback run entirely on-device. No accounts, cloud sign-ins, or telemetry trackers are required. • Native Android Integration: Control sessions seamlessly via interactive Home Screen widgets, persistent status notifications, and a dedicated Quick Settings tile without opening the app. • Minimal Battery Impact: Engineered with a lightweight local architecture to ensure zero unnecessary background battery drain. • Clean Statistics: Review completed intervals, total focus hours, and daily streaks through intuitive, privacy-conscious charts. Whether preparing for exams, managing coding sprints, or practicing structured reading, AuraFocus delivers a quiet, distraction-free environment tailored to your daily routine.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
