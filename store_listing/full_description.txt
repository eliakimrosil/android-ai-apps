Master specialty tea and coffee extraction with precision multi-infusion timing. SteepPulse is an offline brewing assistant designed to track complex steep schedules, water temperatures, and infusion curves without interruptions or accounts. Key Features: • Multi-Infusion Sequences: Track consecutive steeps for Gongfu tea sessions, pour-over bloom phases, and multi-stage extractions with automated interval transitions. • Foreground Brewing Sentinel: Reliable background timing backed by an ongoing system chronometer so you never miss a steep, even when your display turns off. • Quick Controls: Adjust extraction schedules on the fly with instant steep extension buttons directly from your active notifications. • Homescreen Widget and Quick Tile: Monitor active infusion counts, remaining brew duration, and target water temperature directly from your home screen or system status panel. • Offline and Private: All brew logs and custom recipes stay on your device. No account registration, email sign-ups, or analytics trackers required. • Customizable Extraction Profiles: Fine-tune brewing ratios, water heat targets, and extraction timing for loose-leaf teas, espresso puck pre-infusion, and manual drip coffee methods. Designed for coffee roasters, baristas, and loose-leaf tea enthusiasts who demand reliable extraction timing without ads or cloud bloat.
