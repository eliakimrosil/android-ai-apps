CycleGuard provides dedicated cycle tracking and sequence management for your laundry routines. Built with an offline-first architecture, the app manages your multi-stage cycles without requiring accounts, logins, or unnecessary network permissions. Never forget wet clothes in the washing machine again. CycleGuard ensures timely transitions between washing, transferring, drying, and folding through clear, persistent status alerts. KEY FEATURES • Multi-Stage Sequencing: Configure sequential countdowns to track wash cycles, prompt timely drum-to-dryer transfers, monitor drying durations, and schedule folding tasks. • Anti-Mildew Reminders: Receive repeated follow-up reminders when completed cycles are left unattended, keeping laundry fresh and preventing odor buildup. • Persistent Notification Displays: Monitor running timers directly from your notification panel and lock screen with real-time countdown progress. • Quick Settings Tile: Trigger frequent routines with single-tap shortcuts directly from your device quick settings menu. • Custom Cycle Presets: Save personalized presets for standard loads, delicate garments, heavy bedding, and custom machine settings. • Privacy-Focused and Offline: Operates entirely on your device. No cloud account required, no behavioral analytics tracking, and no background network transfers. CycleGuard is built specifically to address the daily friction of laundry management through clean utility and practical device integration.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
