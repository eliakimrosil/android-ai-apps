OmniStream Screen Live Studio delivers professional live broadcasting directly from your Android device. Designed for content creators, mobile gamers, and educators, OmniStream empowers you to stream your display, camera overlay, and audio directly to custom RTMP and RTMPS endpoints with zero cloud intermediaries. High-Performance Direct Streaming OmniStream utilizes hardware-accelerated MediaCodec H.264 video encoding coupled with low-latency MediaProjection capture. Connect straight to your ingest server without third-party proxy relays, preserving video fidelity, reducing latency, and protecting your data privacy. Core Features • Hardware Video Encoding: Efficient H.264/AVC processing engineered to balance bitrate, frame rate, and battery usage. • Dual Video Sources: Broadcast full-screen gameplay or app demonstrations alongside an adjustable front-camera overlay. • Multi-Channel Audio: Capture crisp microphone commentary paired with clear internal system audio mixing. • Standard RTMP & RTMPS Support: Full compatibility with standard port 1935 and TLS-encrypted port 443 streaming URLs. • Real-Time Metrics: Monitor bitrate output, current frame rates, and network stability directly from the in-app dashboard. • Clean Architecture: No background analytics tracking, no intrusive account registrations, and no remote data harvesting. Permissions & Transparency • Screen Capture: Required to capture and broadcast screen activity during an active stream. • Camera: Optional permission utilized solely for the picture-in-picture facecam overlay. • Microphone: Required to broadcast voice commentary and audio during live sessions. Stream your passion with speed, precision, and privacy using OmniStream.
