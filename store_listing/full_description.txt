StealthDrop provides a fast, offline-first ephemeral scratchpad designed for private, distraction-free temporary note-taking. Traditional notepad apps frequently require mandatory cloud logins, disrupt writing with intrusive interstitials, and store unnecessary database trails on your device. StealthDrop takes the opposite approach by prioritizing local-only data control, immediate access, and deliberate privacy. Key Features: • Ephemeral Note Taking: Jot down transient thoughts, codes, or temporary snippets with configurable auto-clear rules and local-only persistence. • Quick Settings Tile: Access your scratchpad instantly from Android Quick Settings without leaving your current workflow. • Floating Overlay Mode: Take rapid notes over any app using an unobtrusive floating window interface designed for multitaskers. • Zero Account Requirement: No registration, no email address, and no sign-in screens. Open the app and begin writing immediately. • Offline by Design: All data remains securely on your local hardware. No remote sync, no telemetry, and no background cloud uploading. • Streamlined Experience: Fast cold-start performance, minimal battery consumption, and a clean interface built for single-purpose productivity. StealthDrop is engineered for journalists, researchers, developers, and privacy-conscious users who need a trustworthy scratchpad that vanishes when the job is done.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
