DoseGuard is a secure, offline medication reminder and pill tracker designed to help you manage your daily prescriptions, vitamins, and refills without compromising your personal health privacy. Unlike traditional health apps that require account creation, cloud syncing, or recurring subscriptions, DoseGuard operates entirely on your device. Your sensitive medical schedules and logs never leave your phone, ensuring complete privacy, zero trackers, and reliable access even without an active internet connection. Key Features: • On-Device Privacy: No account creation or email login required. Your medication data stays encrypted on your local hardware. • Reliable Pill Reminders: Schedule daily, weekly, or custom recurring alerts for prescriptions, supplements, and vitamins. • Quick Action Confirmation: Log doses directly from your notification drawer or Quick Settings tile with a single tap. • Refill Inventory Tracking: Monitor remaining tablet counts automatically and receive low-stock alerts before prescriptions run out. • Flexible Dosage Scheduling: Support for complex regimens, interval dosing, as-needed (PRN) logs, and custom notes per medicine. • Ad-Free Experience: Clean, distraction-free interface built for fast logging without disruptive pop-up ads during critical moments. • Exportable Records: Generate structured intake logs on demand to share directly with your healthcare provider or caregiver. DoseGuard provides a dependable, transparent utility for individuals and caregivers seeking peace of mind through disciplined medication adherence and uncompromising data protection.


Data Safety & Offline Privacy Guarantee:
- 100% Offline: This application operates entirely on your device without requiring an active internet connection.
- Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, monitor, track, store, or transmit any personal identifiable information (PII), device identifiers, or usage telemetry.
- No Accounts or Subscriptions: Enjoy complete utility with zero account creation, logins, or hidden recurring fees.
- Local Storage Only: All items, history, and preferences are retained strictly within on-device sandboxed storage.
