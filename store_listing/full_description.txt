Never miss your transit stop on subways, buses, or trains. SnoozeGuard is a reliable commute wake-up companion designed specifically for public transit commuters navigating underground tunnels, subways, and low-connectivity routes where GPS location signals fail. Traditional transit alarms rely heavily on continuous GPS tracking, which drains battery life, fails completely underground, and causes missed stops. SnoozeGuard solves this by operating entirely offline using motion-assisted timers and periodic vigilance checks to keep you on schedule without location tracking. Key Features: - Tunnel and Subway Ready: Operates offline without requiring GPS, satellite fix, or network connectivity. Perfect for underground transit systems and remote routes. - Dead-Man Vigilance Timer: Optional gentle tactile prompts require a subtle tap or volume-key confirmation to verify you are awake before reaching your final stop. - Tactile and Headphone Audio: Audio routing prioritizes connected headphones or silent haptic vibration patterns, preventing loud accidental speaker alarms in quiet passenger cars. - Quick Settings Tile: Arm or disarm your commute alert directly from the Android Quick Settings panel without opening the full application interface. - Battery Efficient: Built with lightweight local sensors rather than continuous background location polling, preserving power on long trips. - Private and Account-Free: No cloud sign-in, no account registration, and zero tracking analytics. Your commute schedules remain stored on your device. SnoozeGuard provides a dependable, distraction-free travel timer built for everyday transit riders.
