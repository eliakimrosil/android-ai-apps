ThawGuard is an offline food thawing chronometer designed to eliminate guesswork from defrosting meat, poultry, and seafood. Built around recognized food safety guidelines and temperature danger zone curves (40°F to 140°F), ThawGuard provides calculated thaw times based on food type, weight, thickness, and thawing environment. Key Features: • Scientifically Calibrated Thaw Curves: Calculate precise defrost schedules for refrigerator thawing, submerged cold-water baths, and ambient recovery. • Danger Zone Chronometer: Receive high-priority notifications when food approaches critical surface temperature thresholds to help prevent foodborne bacterial proliferation. • Quick Settings Tile Integration: Initiate thaw tracking directly from your Android Quick Settings panel without unlocking your device with messy hands. • Persistent Lock Screen Timer: View elapsed time, remaining duration, and next cold-water replenishment prompts directly from ongoing notifications. • True Offline-First Architecture: Every calculation runs locally on your device. Works seamlessly in walk-in freezers, remote cabins, or kitchens without network reception. • Private by Design: No accounts, no sign-ins, and zero third-party tracking or behavioral analytics. Your kitchen habits remain entirely on your device. Whether preparing weekly meal preps or defrosting poultry for dinner, ThawGuard delivers dependable, straightforward temperature-safe tracking without intrusive interruptions.
