CryoPulse is a dedicated cold exposure and ice bath companion engineered for focus, precision, and privacy. Designed specifically to solve common frustrations with cold plunge timers, CryoPulse delivers essential timing and nervous system regulation tools without forced paywalls, mandatory account creation, or disruptive full-screen ads. Optimized for wet hands and phone-free operation, CryoPulse integrates with Android Quick Settings so you can start, pause, and monitor your session with a single tap straight from your notification shade or lock screen. Key Features: • One-Tap Quick Settings Tile: Launch your cold plunge timer directly from your Android quick settings without unlocking your screen with wet fingers. • Haptic Breathing Pacer: Guided breathwork cadence using silent vibration cues to help maintain parasympathetic control and focus during cold exposure. • Ongoing Lock Screen Controls: Track elapsed time, remaining targets, and current intervals at a glance while your device remains locked. • True Offline Reliability: Complete offline-first functionality ensures your timer never stalls, crashes, or demands internet connectivity by the water. • Battery Efficient Architecture: Minimalist background processing prevents unexpected battery drain during long routines. • Privacy First Design: No mandatory registration, no intrusive account setup, and zero analytics tracking. Whether stepping into an ice bath, performing deliberate cold exposure, or practicing post-workout recovery, CryoPulse offers the precise, distraction-free utility you need.
