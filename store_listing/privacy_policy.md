# Privacy Policy for ClipScrub

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026

ClipScrub ("we", "our", or "the app") is designed strictly with a privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your clipboard, links, and photos are strictly personal and should remain on your device.

### 1. Zero Data Collection & Storage
ClipScrub does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, usage metrics, device identifiers, or shared content. All URL sanitization (tracking parameter removal) and media scrubbing (EXIF and GPS metadata stripping) occur 100% locally on your device.

### 2. No Third-Party Telemetry or Analytics
The app contains:
- Zero third-party SDKs
- Zero tracking cookies
- Zero analytics frameworks (no Google Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Adjust)
- Zero advertising networks

### 3. Network Permissions
ClipScrub operates entirely offline and does not require or use network communication to process your links or media files. No links or photos are ever uploaded to remote servers or third-party proxies.

### 4. Device Permissions
- **Clipboard / Accessibility / Foreground Service (Optional):** Used solely to detect and sanitize tracking parameters in copied URLs locally on your device when enabled by the user.
- **Storage / Media Access:** Used strictly to read and strip EXIF metadata from user-selected images during active sharing intents.

### 5. Policy Updates
Any future updates will uphold our strict guarantee of 100% on-device execution and zero data collection.

### 6. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, contact us at: support@clipscrub.app
