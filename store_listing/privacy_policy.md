# Privacy Policy for PanFlip: Kitchen Sear Timer

*Last Updated: October 2026*

This privacy policy applies to the Android mobile application **PanFlip: Kitchen Sear Timer** (the "Application"), developed and published as a standalone, offline-first mobile utility.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection & Transmission
The Application operates **100% offline** and does not require internet connectivity to perform its core functions.
- **Personal Information:** We do NOT collect, harvest, store, or transmit personal details (such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical locations).
- **Device Identifiers:** We do NOT collect Android Advertising IDs (AAID), IMEI numbers, IP addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
- **Usage Diagnostics:** The Application contains NO third-party analytics SDKs (e.g. Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer) and transmits zero telemetry.

### 2. Local Device Storage
Any user inputs, item records, history entries, or application preferences are stored strictly on your local device within Android's sandboxed storage (`SharedPreferences` / local SQLite database). 
- Your stored data never leaves your device.
- Uninstalling the Application or clearing its app storage via Android Settings permanently deletes all local records.

### 3. Permissions & Device Access
The Application requests only minimal, standard Android platform permissions strictly required to perform local utility operations (such as haptic feedback or local notification reminders). It does not access sensitive runtime permissions (such as Contacts, SMS, Microphone, or Background Location) without your explicit interaction.

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
The Application contains **zero third-party advertisements**, zero ad tracking libraries, and zero data-broker integrations. No data is shared with or sold to third parties under any circumstances.

### 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
The Application does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, the Application complies with global child privacy standards.
