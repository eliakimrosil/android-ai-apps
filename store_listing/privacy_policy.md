# Privacy Policy for TankPulse

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026

TankPulse is built from the ground up with a privacy-first architecture. We believe your vehicle expenses and driving habits are entirely personal.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
TankPulse does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, location data, device identifiers, or financial records. All fuel logs, odometer entries, and expense records are stored strictly in an encrypted local database on your device.

### 2. No Third-Party Trackers or Ads
TankPulse contains zero third-party SDKs, tracking libraries, analytics frameworks, or advertising networks. We do not track your usage or monitor how you interact with the app.

### 3. No Account Required
You are never required to register, create a username, or link social logins to use TankPulse.

### 4. Backups and Export
Any CSV or PDF export files you generate are created locally on your device storage. If you choose to back up your vehicle garage, the backup is saved directly to your local storage or your private personal cloud drive—never to proprietary external servers.

### 5. Contact Us
If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out directly via support@tankpulse.local.
