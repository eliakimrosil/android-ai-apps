# Privacy Policy for DropScribe

**Effective Date:** October 2, 2026

DropScribe ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to complete user privacy. We believe your voice messages, audio notes, and transcripts are strictly personal and confidential.

### 1. 100% On-Device Local Processing
- All speech recognition and audio-to-text processing occurs entirely on your device using local neural network models.
- Audio files and generated text never leave your physical device.
- No audio data is ever transmitted across the internet or stored on external cloud infrastructure.

### 2. Zero Data Collection & Zero Analytics
- We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.
- We do not log usage metrics, IP addresses, device identifiers, or interaction telemetries.
- We do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, Mixpanel) or advertising frameworks.

### 3. Accountless Architecture
- DropScribe does not require user registration, email addresses, phone numbers, or account creation.

### 4. Permissions
- **Storage / Audio Access:** Used strictly to read and process the specific audio file you choose to share with DropScribe via the Android Sharesheet.
- **Network Access:** DropScribe runs completely offline. Network permissions are only utilized if you choose to download optional localized language models or restore a Google Play In-App Purchase.

### 5. Contact
For questions regarding this policy, contact us at: support@dropscribe.app
