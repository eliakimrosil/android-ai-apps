Master meat resting and carryover cooking with precision thermal calculations. RoastRest helps home cooks and pitmasters calculate target internal temperatures and resting durations for steaks, roasts, poultry, and barbecue. When meat is removed from heat, internal temperature continues to rise while moisture redistributes throughout the muscle fibers. RoastRest uses predictive mathematical models to determine exact resting times based on cut thickness, cooking method, probe pull temperature, and target doneness. Key Features: - Predictive Carryover Calculations: Estimate temperature rise after removing meat from grill, oven, or smoker. - Built-in Cut Profiles: Preset configurations for steak cuts, whole poultry, pork roasts, and barbecue briskets. - Large Kitchen-Friendly Controls: High-contrast buttons and expanded touch targets designed for quick operation while cooking. - Quick Settings Tile: Launch or view active resting timers directly from the Android system panel. - Timed Resting Alerts: Reliable device notifications trigger when the resting window completes. - Fully Offline Functionality: Operates completely without internet connectivity, external hardware, or Bluetooth pairing. - Privacy-Focused: No mandatory user accounts, no cloud profile synchronization, and zero background activity tracking.
