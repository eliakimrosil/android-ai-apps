Privacy Policy

Gemini Power Bridge: Lenovo ZUI Power Key Interceptor

Category: Productivity & Utilities • Effective Date: October 2, 2026 • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction & Application Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android mobile application Gemini Power Bridge: Lenovo ZUI Power Key Interceptor (the "Application"), developed and published by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under the KimRosil App Studio portfolio (https://apps.kimrosil.com).

The Application is designed as a standalone, offline-first mobile utility: Hardware key interceptor configuring Lenovo ZUI power button long-press to launch Google Gemini directly.

This policy details how data is handled strictly on your Android device and outlines our adherence to Google Play Store Data Safety and Google AdMob monetization policies.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Local Device Storage

Zero Server Data Collection: We do NOT operate, maintain, or communicate with any remote developer servers or databases to harvest, store, or transmit your personal data.

  • Local Sandboxing: All application inputs, timer logs, calculations, user notes, and configuration settings are stored exclusively on your local device in Android's sandboxed storage (SharedPreferences or local SQLite database).
  • Data Ownership: Your personal data never leaves your device. We do not track, profile, broker, or monetize your personal user records.
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the Application or clearing its storage in Android Settings (Settings → Apps → Gemini Power Bridge: Lenovo ZUI Power Key Interceptor → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage) permanently and irreversibly deletes 100% of local application data.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing independent development and keep this utility completely free of charge, the Application may display digital advertisements served by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Google Mobile Ads SDK Data Collection:

When advertisements are loaded, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) may automatically collect and process certain pseudonymous device and diagnostic telemetry strictly for advertising delivery, contextual frequency capping, fraud prevention, and performance diagnostics:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID) for ad attribution and ad personalization (when enabled).
  • Approximate Location: Non-precise location inferred from your IP address (country or city level) to serve regionally relevant ads.
  • Diagnostics & SDK Performance: Ad interaction events, crash diagnostics, and device characteristics (e.g. device model, Android OS version).

4. Device Permissions Requested by Gemini Power Bridge: Lenovo ZUI Power Key Interceptor

The Application requests only the minimal platform permissions necessary to execute its specific offline utility features on your device:

Permission Reason & On-Device Processing Guarantee

All permission-gated features are executed strictly on your local hardware. No camera feeds, sensor signals, or notification texts are ever uploaded to remote servers.
