MuckCure is a jobsite utility designed for masons, tile setters, and bricklayers to track mortar pot life, initial set windows, and grout cure timelines accurately. Working with cementitious mixtures requires strict timing. Ambient temperature, humidity, and mix ratios alter working windows, leading to premature dry-out, weak bonding, or difficult tile cleanup. MuckCure provides dedicated batch timers configured for masonry and tile applications without distractions.
