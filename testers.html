Frequently Asked Questions

Why is closed testing required?

Google Play developer policy requires new individual developer accounts to test apps with at least 20 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before requesting production publish access.

Is it completely free?

Yes. All KimRosil App Studio applications are 100% free freeware. There are zero subscriptions, paywalls, or paid features.

Are these apps safe and private?

Yes. Every application is built natively with sandboxed local SQLite/SharedPreferences storage. No personal data ever leaves your device.

How do I provide feedback or report bugs?

You can submit feedback directly in the Google Play closed testing track, open an issue on our GitHub repository, or email eliakimrosil@gmail.com.
