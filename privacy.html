Master Developer Privacy Policy

Developer Profile: Eliakim Rosil • KimRosil App Studio • apps.kimrosil.com

Effective Date: October 2, 2026 • Last Updated: October 9, 2026

1. Scope & Developer Identity

This Master Developer Privacy Policy governs all mobile applications ("Applications") developed, published, and maintained by Eliakim Rosil ("we", "us", or "our") under the developer profile KimRosil App Studio, accessible via our official developer portal at https://apps.kimrosil.com and distributed through the Google Play Store and official GitHub releases (https://github.com/eliakimrosil/kimrosil-app-studio).

Our commitment is simple: User privacy by architecture. We design offline-first native Android applications where your personal files, calculations, timers, notes, and activity history remain entirely on your physical device.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Zero Proprietary Server Storage

Zero Developer Server Tracking: We do NOT operate, own, or connect any proprietary remote servers, user tracking databases, or cloud accounts that harvest, store, or profile your personal data.

  • Local Sandboxing: All data entered into our Applications (such as workout logs, fasting timers, fuel entries, receipt scans, notes, and preferences) is stored exclusively in your device's private, sandboxed app directory using Android SharedPreferences, SQLite, or Room database components.
  • No Data Brokering: We do not sell, rent, monetize, trade, or share your personal data with data brokers or marketing firms under any circumstances.
  • Device Isolation: Your personal application records never leave your physical device unless you explicitly initiate an Android system share action (e.g. sharing a sanitized clipboard link).

3. Third-Party Advertising & Monetization (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing independent software development and make our utility applications free for everyone without paid subscriptions, select applications in our portfolio display digital advertisements provided by Google AdMob (a service provided by Google LLC).

Data Processed by Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob):

When you use an ad-supported application, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain pseudonymous device and diagnostic telemetry to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, detect invalid traffic, and protect against fraud:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID) for ad attribution, frequency capping, and ad personalization (when enabled by user).
  • Approximate Location: Non-precise geographical location derived from your IP address (country or city level) to display regionally relevant advertisements.
  • SDK Diagnostics & Telemetry: Ad loading performance, crash reports, device model, Android OS version, and network connection characteristics.

Google's Privacy Policies & Technologies

To learn more about how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review Google's official policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google Advertising Technologies
  • Developer Authorized Sellers (app-ads.txt)

How to Control or Opt-Out of Personalized Advertising

You have full autonomy over your advertising identifiers. You can reset your Google Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time directly through your Android device settings:

  1. Open your Android device Settings.
  2. Navigate to Google → Ads (or on Android 12+, Security & Privacy → Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID or turn on Opt out of Ads Personalization.

When opted out, you will still see contextual advertisements, but they will not be tailored to your prior interests or browsing history.

4. Android Platform Permissions & Hardware Access

Our applications request only the minimal Android platform permissions strictly necessary to execute their respective offline utility features:

Permission Purpose & On-Device Processing Guarantee
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers local countdown, timer expiration, and sentinel alerts. Notification content is never uploaded or shared remotely.
VIBRATE Provides haptic tactile feedback when alarms fire or user actions complete.
WAKE_LOCK Ensures timers and sensor monitors continue running accurately when the device display turns off.
CAMERA Used only in specific utilities (e.g. SnipShelf) for on-device QR scanning or optical character recognition (OCR). Camera frames are analyzed in-memory and are never stored or transmitted.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage Accessed only when you explicitly choose to import, export, or back up local records.

5. User Data Retention & Right to Erasure (Complete Deletion)

100% User-Controlled Deletion: Because all application data resides strictly on your physical Android hardware, you have unconditional control over its retention.

  • Clear Application Storage: Open Android Settings → Apps → select the Application → Storage & Cache → tap Clear Storage / Clear Data. This permanently and irrevocably deletes all local records, database entries, and preferences.
  • Uninstall Application: Uninstalling the Application from your device automatically purges the application's sandboxed storage directory.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our applications are designed as general-audience utilities and do not target or knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area / UK). If you believe an application has inadvertently received personal data from a child, please contact us immediately at eliakimrosil@gmail.com so we can take prompt corrective steps.

7. International Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, California, or another jurisdiction with applicable privacy regulations:

  • Right to Access & Portability: Since all data is stored locally in standard formats on your device, you have immediate access to your records at all times.
  • Right to Erasure: You can erase all data instantly via Android Settings as described above.
  • Consent for Advertising: Where required by the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Google's EU User Consent Policy, our ad-supported applications present a certified Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog allowing you to freely grant, refuse, or manage ad consent.

8. Security Safeguards

Your local data is protected by Android's underlying Linux application sandboxing architecture, ensuring no other application on your device can access your private database or preference files. Any network communication required by third-party advertising SDKs (AdMob) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).
