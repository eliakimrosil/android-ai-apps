Privacy Policy

IronPulse: Offline Workout Program & Gym Tracker

Last updated: October 2026

1. Zero Data Collection Guarantee

IronPulse operates 100% offline. The application does not collect, transmit, sell, or monitor any personal information, workout logs, device identifiers, or biometric data.

2. Local Device Storage Only

All your routines, exercise history, weight logs, and timer settings are stored strictly in your device's internal private storage (SharedPreferences) and never leave your hardware.

3. Third-Party Libraries & Network Access

IronPulse contains zero third-party tracking SDKs, zero advertising networks, and requires no internet permission (android.permission.INTERNET is not requested).
